# SAUT — Brand Context

## Purpose

This document supplies brand and product context. It does not redefine visual tokens. Exact visual values live in the four canonical SAUT design-system files at repository root.

## Brand

SAUT is a streetwear clothing brand centered on oversized garments, original SAUT designs and customer personalization.

The digital product must make commerce and customization feel like one coherent brand experience.

The logo is a fixed brand asset. Do not redraw, re-typeset, distort, rotate or stylistically replace it during UI work.

## Product world

Use the real product as the primary source of visual ideas:

- garment silhouette;
- oversized fit;
- colorway;
- artwork and print;
- front/back garment views;
- size and availability;
- product photography;
- collections and drops;
- customization;
- placement and scale of customer artwork;
- real production constraints when the product provides them.

Do not invent technical specifications, scarcity, materials, sustainability claims, measurements or production capabilities for visual effect.

## Character

SAUT should feel:

- direct;
- confident;
- contemporary;
- visual;
- editorial when presenting campaigns;
- precise when presenting product and customization decisions.

It must not become a generic SaaS interface, dashboard aesthetic or anonymous ecommerce template.

## Copy

Prefer short, concrete retail language tied to the action:

- Ver colección
- Elegir talla
- Personalizar prenda
- Agregar al carrito
- Ver detalles
- Frente / Espalda
- Ajustar diseño
- Finalizar compra

Avoid filler that could belong to any fashion brand.

## Photography

Photography is a primary brand surface.

Prioritize:

- clear garment silhouette;
- material and print detail;
- deliberate crops;
- accurate product color;
- strong scale when campaign imagery calls for it;
- media that helps the customer understand fit or customization.

Do not use generic stock imagery or effects that make the real garment harder to judge.

## Color authority

Do not copy color values from this document. Use the canonical values in:

- `saut-DESIGN.md`
- `saut-tokens.json`
- `saut-variables.css`
- `saut-theme.css`

This prevents palette drift.
